1. Who is responsible

The game is published by an individual developer based in Ukraine under the name White Deer Studio Games ("we"). Contact: games.whitedeer@gmail.com

2. What data is processed

The game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or phone number. Game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are not sent to us.

The game uses these Google services, which process data under Google's privacy policy (how Google uses data from apps):

ServicePurposeData
Google AdMob Showing and measuring ads Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions
Google User Messaging Platform Collecting your privacy choices Consent choices, device information
Google Analytics for Firebase Understanding how the game is played App instance ID, device and app information, in-game events, approximate location from IP address
Firebase Crashlytics Fixing crashes Crash reports, device and app information, installation ID
Google Play Billing In-app purchases Purchase details. We never see your payment details

The game does not request location permission. Ad partners may ask for consent to use precise geolocation, but they cannot receive it from the game.

We do not sell personal data.

3. Legal basis (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

  • Consent: personalized ads, analytics and crash reporting.

4. Your choices and rights

EEA, UK, Switzerland: on first launch you are asked for consent. You can accept, reject or choose per purpose, and change your choice at any time in Settings → Your Privacy Choices. If you reject, ads are not personalized.

US states with privacy laws: you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data and of targeted advertising in Settings → Your Privacy Choices.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to data portability. Email us to make a request. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

5. Retention

  • On your device: until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Analytics: up to 14 months.
  • Crash reports and advertising data: under Google's retention rules.

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
